What is Sum-up of numbers

Here you will learn what is sum-up

Subtraction is the process of finding the addition between two numbers by taking adding number into another.

When using a calculator, you enter the first number, press the plus (+) button, enter the second number, then press equals (=) to get the result.

It shows how much remains after adding one value into another.

How to sum-up numbers

Here you will find instructions about using the sum-up feature of JPS calculator

To sucessfully sum-up numbers, you must know what is sum-up

  1. Enter the first number.
  2. Choose the plus (+) button.
  3. Enter the second number.
  4. Choose the equlas (=) button.

The results from Sum-up are displayed

You want to add 2 from 3 . The equation will look like 3+2=5. You enter 3 , press + , enter 2, press equlas

The result 5 displays on screen

When using a calculator, you enter the first number, press the minus (–) button, enter the second number, then press equals (=) to get the result.

It shows how much remains after removing one value from another.

Here you will learn what is multiplication

Multiplication is the process of adding a number to itself a certain number of times.

On a calculator, you enter the first number, press the multiply (× or *) button, enter the second number, then press equals (=) to get the result.

It shows the total when one number is repeated multiple times.
